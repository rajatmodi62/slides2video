slides2video 

tired of making videos for your papers?
use this. 

just make a ppt, add your slide notes, and this code will take care of the rest. 

`python slides_to_video.py ffn.pptx --out ffn.mp4`

faq:

why use this if it is ai generated?

i am all for human explained stuff. two issues. 

- humans take shortcuts, and don't like explaining things in more detail. 
- agents make pretty good slides now. 
- i like to reserve my energy for in-person explanation at conference.
- there is only finite time, and a lot of work to do , so i like taking shortcuts.
